# PRD: Sumit Kumar Pandey Portfolio
## Goal
Fast single-page public portfolio for recruiters, donors and government partners, with one-click resume download.
## Requirements
1. Sticky themed menu (white text), hamburger on mobile, scroll-spy highlight.
2. Light and dark theme toggle.
3. Multi-page routes: dashboard Home, Experience, Expertise, Impact, Education, Contact. Each page shares the SP header/footer and supports direct navigation.
4. Colorful card dashboard with glass surfaces, gradient accents, carousels, colorful metrics, Lucide icons, and SP monogram branding.
5. Resume PDF download above the footer and in hero, with "Last updated" label.
6. Contact privacy: phone behind reveal button; email decoded client-side.
7. SEO and Vercel Analytics. Back-to-top button. Print stylesheet.
8. Footer developer credit line.
## Success metrics
Lighthouse 90+; no horizontal scroll from 320px; PDF downloads on all devices.
## Out of scope
Contact form, Hindi content, recommendations, publications, blog, CMS.
